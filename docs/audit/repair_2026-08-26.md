# Repair and freeze — control window overlap

**2026-08-26. Scope-limited repair. No open-ended auditing.**

One defect fixed, everything downstream recomputed, the suites re-run once, and
the result frozen. Further investigation now happens only when a test fails, a
number becomes inconsistent, or live data gives a concrete reason.

**Headline numbers: 59 build assertions pass. The archive sensitivity floor
moved from d = 0.5 to d = 1.0.**

---

## 1. The repair

Control anchors were selected at one-minute spacing, so 30-minute control
windows overlapped their neighbours by 29 of 30 minutes. Every statistic
weighted by the control count treated them as independent observations.

**Fix.** Control candidates are thinned to a minimum separation equal to the
window length (30 minutes) **before** hour matching, not after pooling.
Thinning after pooling would let per-event selections overlap each other and
would hide how small the real supply of independent controls is.

Applied in both paths, so they cannot diverge on this again:

| file | change |
|---|---|
| the frozen analysis implementation | `thin_non_overlapping()`, `assert_non_overlapping()`, thinning inside `build_windows()` |
| `exploration/audit_independent.py` | same rule in `build_controls()` |

**The assertion makes the class of defect structurally impossible.**
`assert_non_overlapping()` raises — it does not warn — if any two control
anchors sit closer than the window length, and `build_windows()` calls it on
every pooled control set before returning. Verified both directions: it fires on
anchors 20 minutes apart and passes at 30.

`build_windows()` now also reports `n_candidate_minutes_overlapping`,
`control_separation_min`, `events_meeting_min_controls` and a verdict that names
undersupply explicitly, so a future run cannot quietly fall back to a degraded
match.

### Precision figures — unaffected

The chance-precision floor is event-adjacent minutes divided by eligible
minutes. It does not reference the control set, so the precision and lift
figures are unchanged by this repair. Stated because "recompute everything that
assumed 744 controls" could otherwise be read as leaving them unchecked.

### One thing the repair made worse, stated plainly

**Hour matching is destroyed.** With 39 independent windows spread across 24
hours, no event can find 20 same-hour controls: strict matches fall from 24 to
**0**, and the median tolerance widens from ≤2 h to 7 h. The repair fixes the
independence defect and re-exposes the time-of-day confound that §5 of the
research note is about.

These two constraints are in genuine tension and four days of data cannot
satisfy both. That is a data-quantity problem, not a code problem, and it is not
resolved here.

## 3. Contract impact — reported, not acted on

**The hashed contract is untouched.** `EVALUATION_CONTRACT.md` still hashes to
`505b12d0999665230127fcc630aeabe40a83aefdb78f07359a1ecd176c291869`.

### The primary test does not depend on this

The contract's primary score is out-of-sample CRPS from a Gaussian
location–scale regression fit **on anchors** (§1.6). It does not use an
event-versus-control comparison. Controls enter the contract only in:

- §2.11, matched-control quality, a mandatory report item
- §3, SUSPICIOUS, "control matching quality is outside its declared tolerance"
- §6.4, re-running the meta-test at realised event and control counts

**The repair therefore does not invalidate the pre-registered primary test.** It
changes the secondary AUC/Cohen's-d diagnostic, the placebo, and the sensitivity
floor quoted beside every null.

### How many independent controls the rule actually yields

Control supply falls as event density rises — every event blocks its 30-minute
pre-window plus a 60-minute lookahead.

| source | events/day | independent windows/day |
|---|---:|---:|
| theoretical ceiling (1,440 / 30) | — | 48 |
| archive, 3 analysed days | 19 | **13** |
| live capture, BTC 2026-08-23 | 6 | **34** |

### Can the live capture supply enough at ≥30 TEST events?

| scenario | days to 75 events | independent controls accumulated |
|---|---:|---:|
| at the live rate (6 events/day) | ~12.5 | ~425 |
| at the archive rate (19 events/day) | ~3.9 | ~51 |

**On count, yes — comfortably at the live rate.** The binding constraint is not
the total but the **hour distribution**: ~425 windows spread over 24 hours gives
roughly 18 per hour, which is near but below the contract's 20-per-event
requirement for strict same-hour matching. Same-hour matching becomes possible
somewhere around two to three weeks of live capture, not at the 75-event
threshold itself if events arrive quickly.

### Does this change the effective sensitivity floor?

**Yes, and it is the number that matters most.** Re-measured by planting known
signals into the 57-event archive, 40 trials per effect size, 200 placebo draws,
BH against the 120-test background:

| controls | d = 0.3 | d = 0.5 | d = 0.8 | d = 1.0 | floor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 744 overlapping | 38% | 80% | 100% | 100% | **d = 0.5** |
| 39 non-overlapping | 2% | 20% | 75% | 82% | **d = 1.0** |

All-three rates (detect ∧ BH ∧ placebo). Negative control at d = 0: 5% detect,
**0%** all-three — correct.

**The floor doubled.** Nothing about the market changed; the earlier figure
counted each control window about 23 times.

### Options, for your decision

1. **Do nothing.** The contract is unaffected on its primary score. Quote the
   repaired floor beside every null and re-measure at the realised counts, which
   §6.4 already requires.
2. **Relax `min_controls` below 20** for the secondary diagnostic, accepting
   wider hour tolerance. Requires a contract amendment and a new hash.
3. **Raise the event threshold** so control supply accumulates. Also an
   amendment.
4. **Drop the secondary diagnostic** and rely on CRPS alone. The cleanest option
   and the largest change.

I have taken none of them.

### What changed, in one paragraph

Control windows overlapped, which inflated every control-weighted statistic by
roughly a factor of three. After repair, BH survivors on the archive fall from
25 to 3, all three in the bin adjacent to the move; no early-bin cell survives
anywhere; both liquidation cells fail BH; and the sensitivity floor doubles from
d = 0.5 to d = 1.0. The pre-registered primary test is unaffected because it
scores CRPS on anchors rather than comparing events to controls. Hour matching
is now impossible on four days of data, which is a data-quantity problem this
repair does not solve.

**Investigation stops here** until a test fails, a number becomes inconsistent,
or live data gives a concrete reason.
